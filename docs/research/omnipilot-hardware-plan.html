Strategy — The "Why & Where"

1.1 The Hardware Vision

OmniPilot started as an AI voice assistant for productivity — a software companion that helps you think, remember, and act. But voice AI becomes transformative when paired with purpose-built hardware. A $10 BLE pendant streaming audio to your phone. A camera-equipped device guiding a blind person through a busy Indian street. A voice module letting a farmer command a crop-dusting drone in Hindi.

Core Insight

Software AI is a crowded race to the bottom. Hardware AI creates defensible moats — certification barriers, supply chain relationships, form factor IP, and distribution partnerships that software-only players cannot replicate overnight.

The hardware extension turns OmniPilot from a voice app into an assistive AI platform — one that can serve the visually impaired, agriculture, wearable computing, and smart home markets through a unified AI brain with specialized peripherals.

ADIP Cost Coverage

India has the world's largest visually impaired population. The existing assistive devices — OrCam MyEye (Rs 2.5L), Envision Glasses (Rs 3.5L) — are priced for Western markets. OmniPilot Lite at Rs 4,999 is 50x cheaper.

ADIP Scheme — The Game Changer

The Government of India's ADIP (Assistance to Disabled Persons) scheme covers 100% of assistive device costs under Rs 15,000. OmniPilot Lite at Rs 4,999 qualifies automatically. This means zero cost to the end user — the government pays. Tenders are placed through NGOs for 500–5,000 units at a time.

80/20 On-Device Rule

80% of daily tasks (obstacle alerts, text reading, object identification) run entirely on-device with zero latency and zero API cost. Only complex scene description and navigation use cloud LLMs. This keeps the monthly API cost under Rs 50/user.

3.3 Drone Voice Integration

Indian agriculture is rapidly adopting drones for crop spraying, but pilots need extensive training to operate them. Voice control removes the training barrier: a farmer speaks in Hindi, and the drone follows.

Safety-First Design

Voice-controlled drones require triple safety layers: (1) Geofence boundaries that cannot be overridden by voice, (2) Command whitelist — only pre-approved commands accepted, (3) Dead man's switch — drone hovers/lands if voice link drops for >5 seconds.

3.4 Smart Home (Software-Only, Phase 5)

Smart home integration is a software-only play — no custom hardware needed. OmniPilot's voice AI connects to existing smart home ecosystems through established protocols.

IntegrationCoverageEffort
Tuya API80% of Indian smart home devices (WiFi bulbs, plugs, cameras)2–3 weeks SDK integration
HomeKit / MatterApple ecosystem (Thread, WiFi)4–6 weeks certification
Google HomeGoogle Nest ecosystem2–3 weeks API integration
Alexa Skills KitAmazon Echo ecosystem2–3 weeks skill development
Why Phase 5?

Smart home integration is low effort, high retention — once a user controls their home through OmniPilot, switching costs are high. But it is not a differentiator for initial adoption. Ship it after the core hardware products establish the user base.
