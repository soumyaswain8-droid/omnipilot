1. Executive Summary

OmniPilot is a personal AI assistant inspired by Omi AI — an always-listening, always-remembering companion that works across desktop, WhatsApp, and mobile. Unlike Omi (hardware pendant) or Humane (failed AI Pin), OmniPilot is software-only and local-first — everything runs on your Mac/phone, no data leaves your device.

The Big Opportunity
OmniPilot isn't just another AI assistant. It becomes the unified voice interface across the entire Sidewall product portfolio — BizBot, AdPilot, QuickBillPro, TradePilot, DevPilot, SetlIn, and DesignPilot. One assistant to manage all your businesses, investments, and life.

Key Findings

Key Insight
  • Hardware AI is dead. Humane Pin bricked Feb 2025. Rabbit R1 has 95% abandonment. Software-first wins.
  • India market is perfect — WhatsApp-dominant culture, 65% phones can run on-device AI, Rs 399-500/mo pricing.
  • BizBot is the killer integration (95% synergy) — voice-to-WhatsApp for business owners is the hook.
  • Revenue: Rs 2.1 Cr ARR potential at just 100 users across the portfolio.
  • Tech stack is ready — Whisper + Ollama + SQLite all run on your Mac today. Zero cloud needed.
  • Your moat — no standalone assistant has 7 integrated products feeding one brain.

India Advantages

  • WhatsApp is the platform — 78% of SMBs already use it for business. That's our distribution channel.

Pricing Reality
Above Rs 2,000/month ($24), adoption drops sharply outside enterprise. The $5-10/month range is the sweet spot for India. Free tiers are table stakes (Google/Jio partnership set this expectation).

What Winners Got Right

Success Patterns
  • Software-first beats hardware-first — Limitless, Notion healthy. Humane, Rabbit failed.
  • Meeting intelligence is the beachhead — Every winner started with "make meetings useful"
  • Sub-$100 hardware ceiling — $89 Omi sells. $199+ struggles. $699 dies.
  • Phone is the real competitor — Must be 10x better than a phone app, not 10x worse

How It Works

You speak | v [Silero VAD] — Is someone talking? | | Yes No → discard (saves battery) | v [Whisper] — Speech to text (200ms per chunk on M1) | v [SQLite Memory] — Store with timestamp, people, topics | v [Ollama LLM] — Summarize, extract action items, answer questions | v [You get] — Daily summaries, reminders, answers

Five Layers of Defensibility

Competitive Advantages
  1. Proprietary product graph — Deep integrations with 7 products spanning ads, billing, communication, trading, design
  2. Shared cross-domain context — Knows your invoice AND campaign AND trade position. Context compounds
